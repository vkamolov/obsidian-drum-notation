Privacy policy

Your score is not sent to the developer.

Effective 17 August 2026

Plugin processing

The Drum Notation Importer plugin makes no network requests, uses no developer-operated service, includes no telemetry, and sends no score, notation, or report to Viktor Kamolov. Its bundled notation validator runs locally and is network-free.

Host agent processing

The ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, Gemini, or other host you use handles images and prompts under that host’s own data policy. The importer provides instructions to the host; it does not independently upload attachments or receive a copy of them.

Verification playground

Source images, focused crops, pasted responses, import reports, and review state used in the verification workspace are ephemeral and stay in the current browser tab. Saving from that workspace stores normalized notation only. The playground makes no provider API call and contains no tracking or analytics.

Support

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. Information posted there is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not post copyrighted scores, personal data, credentials, or other material you do not have permission to share.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the importer support form.
